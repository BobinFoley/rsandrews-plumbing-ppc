## Why Metro Atlanta Homeowners Switch to Tankless

- **Endless Hot Water on Demand:** Heats water instantaneously as it flows through the heat exchanger—never wait 45 minutes for a storage tank to recover.
- **Lower Monthly Energy Bills:** Eliminates standby heat loss by only firing when a hot water tap opens, saving up to 34% on water-heating energy costs.
- **Twice the Lifespan:** Quality tankless systems last 20+ years with annual descaling, compared to 8–12 years for conventional storage tanks.
- **Compact Wall-Mounted Footprint:** Frees up valuable basement, garage, or utility closet space and eliminates the risk of a 50-gallon tank bursting indoors.
