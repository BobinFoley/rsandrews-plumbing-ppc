## Common Causes of Main Sewer Line Failure

- **Tree Root Intrusion:** Mature Georgia oaks, pines, and maples send roots directly into tiny joints of older clay or cast-iron sewer pipes.
- **Pipe Corrosion & Cracks:** Aging cast-iron and Orangeburg sewer lines rust through, flake internally, and collapse over time.
- **Bellied or Shifted Pipes:** Settling Georgia red clay soil creates low spots where waste and grease accumulate repeatedly.
- **Severe Grease & Debris Buildup:** Years of cooking oil, flushable wipes, and household debris narrowing the main drain line.
