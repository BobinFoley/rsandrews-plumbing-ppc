Routine preventative maintenance catches minor leaks, failing valves, and sediment buildup before they lead to emergency water damage. **Call [404-806-9284](tel:4048069284)** to schedule an annual whole-home plumbing inspection with an RS Andrews Master Plumber.

## What a Plumbing Inspection Includes

- **Water Pressure Testing:** Checking home static pressure to ensure it does not exceed 80 PSI (which ruptures pipes and appliances).
- **Water Heater Flush & Check:** Flushing mineral sediment, testing the temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve, and inspecting the anode rod.
- **Supply Lines & Valves:** Inspecting washing machine hoses, toilet shutoffs, and under-sink angle stops for corrosion.
- **Drain & Trap Inspection:** Checking P-traps, kitchen disposals, and main cleanouts for sluggish flow.
