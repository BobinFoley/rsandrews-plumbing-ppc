Gas line work demands strict code compliance, precision pressure testing, and licensed Master Plumber expertise. Whether you smell gas near an appliance, need a county permit pressure test after a utility shutoff, or want a new gas line run for a stove, grill, or tankless water heater, **call RS Andrews at [404-806-9284](tel:4048069284)**.

> **If you smell rotten eggs or suspect an active gas leak:** Leave the house immediately, avoid flipping light switches or using flames, and call your gas utility emergency line or 911 from outside. Once safe, call RS Andrews at **404-806-9284** to repair and restore your gas piping.

## Certified Gas Piping Services

- **Gas Leak Detection & Repair:** Electronic combustible gas detectors and pressure drop testing to pinpoint and permanently seal leaks in black iron, CSST, or underground gas lines.
- **Utility Red-Tag & Pressure Test Restoration:** If Atlanta Gas Light or your municipality locked your meter due to a leak, we perform the required repair, pull local permits, pass county inspection, and get your gas turned back on fast.
- **New Appliance Gas Lines:** Dedicated gas runs for kitchen ranges, tankless water heaters, clothes dryers, fireplaces, outdoor kitchens, built-in grills, pool heaters, and standby generators.
- **Gas Shutoff Valve Upgrades:** Replacing stiff or leaking brass valves with safe, modern quarter-turn gas shutoffs.
