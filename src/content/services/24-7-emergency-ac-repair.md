Plumbing and home system emergencies can strike at any hour. A burst water pipe, failed water heater, or backed-up sewer line requires immediate attention to protect your home. **Call [404-806-9284](tel:4048069284)** immediately to describe your situation and have an emergency technician dispatched in the Metro Atlanta area.

## Immediate Emergency Support

- If water is flooding your home, shut off the main water valve immediately.
- If an electric water heater is leaking, turn off its circuit breaker.
- For complete emergency plumbing details and guidance, see our dedicated [24/7 Emergency Plumber](/24-7-emergency-plumbing/) page.

## What Happens Next

An RS Andrews technician will arrive in a fully equipped service vehicle, diagnose the cause of the problem, and explain your repair options with upfront flat-rate pricing before beginning work.

For drain issues, see [drain cleaning](/drain-cleaning/). For hot water issues, see [water heater repair](/water-heater-repair/).
