## Signs Your Home Needs Repiping

- **Polybutylene ("Quest" Gray) Pipes:** Common in Metro Atlanta homes built between 1978 and 1995, polybutylene reacts with municipal chlorine and ruptures unexpectedly—and many insurers require replacement.
- **Galvanized Steel Pipes:** Corrode from the inside out, choking water pressure and turning tap water rusty or discolored.
- **Repeated Pinhole Leaks in Copper:** Once aging copper lines start developing multiple leaks, full replacement is safer and more economical than constant emergency repairs.
- **Temperature Drops in the Shower:** Flushing a toilet causes scalding or freezing shifts in shower temperature due to restricted supply lines.
