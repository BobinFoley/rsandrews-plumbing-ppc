## Our Drain Clearing Solutions

- **Motorized Snake & Rooter Equipment:** Powerful professional augers cut through dense hair clogs, soap scum, and foreign objects trapped in kitchen, bath, and shower drains.
- **High-Pressure Hydro Jetting:** Blasts high-velocity water (up to 4,000 PSI) through drain lines to completely scrub away decades of hardened grease, scale buildup, and stubborn tree roots without digging up your yard.
- **High-Definition Sewer Camera Inspection:** We insert a fiber-optic video camera through your drain cleanout to locate cracks, root intrusion, pipe bellies, or collapsed sections and verify the line is 100% clear.
- **Mainline Sewer Services:** From city cleanouts to whole-home line repairs, we keep your main waste pipes flowing cleanly.

## Safe for Pipes, Tough on Clogs

Store-bought chemical drain cleaners often corrode older pipes and produce dangerous fumes without permanently fixing the clog. Our plumbers use mechanical and hydraulic tools that protect your plumbing system while providing a lasting solution.
