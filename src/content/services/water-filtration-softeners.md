## Water Quality Solutions for Georgia Homes

- **Whole-Home Carbon Filtration:** Removes chlorine taste and odor, sediment, and chemical byproducts at the main water entry point so every shower, sink, and appliance receives filtered water.
- **Water Softeners & Scale Conditioners:** Eliminates hard-water calcium and magnesium buildup that clogs showerheads, shortens tankless water heater life, and leaves chalky residue on fixtures.
- **Under-Sink Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems:** Dedicated multi-stage drinking water purification right at your kitchen sink and refrigerator ice maker.
- **Pressure Reducing Valves (PRVs):** Often paired with water treatment installs to keep incoming municipal pressure safely below 80 PSI and protect appliances.
