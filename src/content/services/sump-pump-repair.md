## Signs Your Sump or Ejector Pump Needs Service

- **Pump Runs Continuously or Never Turns On:** Stuck float switches, failed check valves, or burned-out motors.
- **Loud Grinding, Rattling, or Vibrating Noises:** Worn bearings or jammed impellers that are close to seizing completely.
- **Musty Smells or Standing Water in the Pit:** Sluggish discharge lines or sewage ejector seals starting to fail.
- **Pump Is Over 7–10 Years Old:** Replacing an aging pump proactively before storm season prevents thousands in flooded-basement restoration costs.
