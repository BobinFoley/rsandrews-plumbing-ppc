Plumbing emergencies cannot wait. A burst pipe, failed water heater, or sewer backing up into your living space can cause thousands of dollars in water damage within hours. **Call [404-806-9284](tel:4048069284)** immediately to speak with a dispatcher and rush an emergency plumber to your Metro Atlanta home or business.

## Immediate Steps While the Plumber Is Dispatched

1. **Locate your main water shutoff valve:** Turn the valve clockwise to stop incoming water pressure immediately.
2. **Turn off power to electric water heaters:** If your water heater is leaking or empty, switch off the breaker to prevent heating element burnout or electrical hazards.
3. **Avoid chemical drain cleaners:** Harsh chemical cleaners generate caustic heat and toxic fumes that damage pipes and endanger technicians.
