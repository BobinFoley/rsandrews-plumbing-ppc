## Repair or Replace?

- **When to Repair:** If your unit is under 8 years old and the problem involves a faulty thermocouple, heating element, thermostat, pressure relief valve, or burner assembly, repair is typically the most economical solution.
- **When to Replace:** If your water heater is 10+ years old, rusty, or leaking from the tank body itself, replacement is necessary to prevent severe flood damage.

## Tankless Water Heater Upgrades

Tired of running out of hot water during back-to-back morning showers? Upgrade to an energy-efficient tankless water heater:
- **Endless Hot Water:** Heats water on demand as it flows through the unit.
- **Lower Energy Bills:** Uses up to 34% less energy by eliminating standby heat loss.
- **Space-Saving Wall Mount:** Frees up floor space in basements, closets, or utility rooms.
- **Longer Lifespan:** Up to 20+ years of reliable service compared to 8–12 years for storage tanks.
