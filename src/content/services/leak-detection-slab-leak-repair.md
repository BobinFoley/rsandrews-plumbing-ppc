## Warning Signs of a Hidden Water or Slab Leak

- **Sudden Spike in Your Water Bill:** Unexplained increases in monthly water usage when your household habits haven't changed.
- **Warm Spots on Floors:** Hot water line leaks beneath concrete slabs frequently create warm patches on tile, hardwood, or carpet.
- **Sound of Running Water:** Hearing water hissing or trickling in walls or floors when every faucet and appliance is turned off.
- **Damp Drywall, Musty Odors, or Low Water Pressure:** Moisture wicking up baseboards, mildew smells, or a noticeable drop in shower pressure.
