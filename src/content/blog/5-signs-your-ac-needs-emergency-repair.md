Plumbing issues can turn from minor annoyances into catastrophic property damage in a matter of minutes. In Metro Atlanta, sudden temperature drops in winter, aging cast iron or galvanized pipes, and hidden slab leaks put continuous stress on residential plumbing systems.

Recognizing the early warning signs of an urgent plumbing emergency can save you thousands of dollars in water damage restoration and prevent hazardous mold growth.

## 1. Multiple Drains Backing Up Simultaneously

If plunging one toilet causes dirty water or sewage to gurgle up into your bathtub, shower, or adjacent sink, you are dealing with a main sewer line blockage. 

A mainline backup means waste water has nowhere to go and will quickly overflow through the lowest fixtures in your home. Stop running all water, avoid flushing toilets, and call an emergency plumber immediately. RS Andrews uses motorized augers and hydro jetting to clear main sewer lines fast.

## 2. Unexplained Water Pressure Drop Across the Whole House

A sudden, sharp drop in water pressure throughout every faucet and shower in your house indicates a serious supply line problem. 

Common causes include:
- A major burst water pipe hidden in a wall, crawlspace, or basement
- A damaged main underground water service line between the water meter and your foundation
- A ruptured pressure reducing valve (PRV)

Check your water meter: if the leak indicator dial is spinning rapidly while all faucets are turned off, water is flooding somewhere inside or outside your home.

## 3. Water Pooling Around the Water Heater Base

If you see pooling water beneath your gas or electric water heater, take action immediately. 

While a dripping temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve or drain valve can sometimes be repaired, a water heater tank that has rusted through can burst without warning, dumping 40 to 80 gallons of scalding water directly onto your flooring. Turn off the cold water shutoff valve above the heater, switch off the power or gas supply, and call RS Andrews for same-day water heater replacement.

## 4. The Sound of Running Water When All Faucets Are Off

Hearing faint hissing, rushing, or dripping sounds behind walls, underneath floorboards, or beneath a concrete slab is a classic sign of a hidden pressurized water leak.

Slab leaks can quietly erode the soil beneath your foundation, cause cracks in drywall and tile, and cause utility bills to skyrocket. RS Andrews utilizes non-invasive electronic acoustic sensors and thermal imaging to pinpoint the exact leak location without tearing up your home.

## 5. Foul Sewage Odors Inside Your Home

If your bathrooms, laundry room, or kitchen smell strongly of rotten eggs or raw sewage, dangerous sewer gases (including methane and hydrogen sulfide) are entering your living spaces. 

This usually happens when:
- A drain trap has cracked or dried out
- A toilet wax ring seal has degraded and broken
- A rooftop plumbing vent pipe is obstructed
- The main sewer pipe is cracked or separated beneath your house
