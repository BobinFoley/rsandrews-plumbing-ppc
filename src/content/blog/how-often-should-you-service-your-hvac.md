Homeowners frequently ask our licensed plumbers: *“Do I really need to have my plumbing inspected if nothing is currently leaking?”*

The short answer is **yes—at least once a year**. In Metro Atlanta, high municipal water pressure (often exceeding 80–100 PSI), seasonal temperature fluctuations, and invasive tree roots in clay sewer pipes make annual preventative plumbing checks the most effective way to protect your home from sudden, catastrophic water damage.

## Key Checkpoints of an Annual Plumbing Inspection

A comprehensive plumbing audit by an RS Andrews Master Plumber focuses on components that are vulnerable to silent wear:

### 1. Water Pressure Testing
Municipal water suppliers in Georgia often pump water at very high pressure to reach fire hydrants and hilly terrain. If your home's Pressure Reducing Valve (PRV) fails, incoming water pressure can spike past 80 PSI, silently stressing pipe joints, toilet fill valves, and flexible appliance supply lines until they burst.

### 2. Water Heater Flush and Safety Inspection
- **Sediment Removal:** Georgia water contains dissolved minerals that settle at the bottom of the tank, forming a hard insulating layer that causes the metal to overheat and crack.
- **T&P Relief Valve Test:** Ensuring the temperature and pressure relief valve opens freely and doesn't stick.
- **Anode Rod Check:** The sacrificial anode rod attracts corrosive elements away from the steel tank walls. Replacing a depleted anode rod can add 5+ years to the life of your water heater.

### 3. Supply Lines and Shutoff Valves
- Testing emergency shutoff valves under every sink, toilet, and washing machine.
- Inspecting flexible stainless-steel braided hoses for corrosion, kinks, or bulging.

### 4. Drain & Sewer Cleanout Health
- Checking flow rates in high-use drains (kitchen, showers, laundry).
- Verifying exterior sewer cleanouts are accessible, capped, and free of root intrusion.

## Simple Preventative Steps Homeowners Can Do

Between professional visits, you can safeguard your home plumbing:
- **Never pour cooking oil or grease down the kitchen sink:** Wipe pans with paper towels before washing.
- **Test your toilet flappers:** Put a few drops of food coloring into the toilet tank. If color appears in the bowl within 15 minutes without flushing, your flapper is leaking water.
- **Know your main water shutoff valve:** Ensure every adult in your household knows where the main valve is located and can turn it off quickly in an emergency.
